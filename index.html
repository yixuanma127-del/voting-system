Diagnosis

Five Problems. Five Solutions.

A systematic audit of Casetify's current marketing mix reveals clear opportunities for improvement.

Problem
📱

Product Line Stagnation

The product range hasn't evolved to meet the growing demand for sustainability. Casetify's core strength — phone cases — needs expansion into complementary accessories with eco-conscious materials.

Solution
♻️

Sustainable Expansion

Introduce biodegradable and recycled-material cases. Expand accessories line: AirPods cases, laptop sleeves, MagSafe wallets — all with the same design-forward DNA that defines Casetify.

Problem
⭐

Declining CSAT: 95% → 91%

Customer satisfaction dropped 4 percentage points by February 2024. In the premium segment, service experience directly impacts repurchase intent and word-of-mouth.

Solution
🚀

Target: CSAT ≥ 95%

Implement a proactive customer service model: faster response times, hassle-free returns, and a post-purchase follow-up system. Launch a customer feedback loop integrated into product development.

Problem
💵

Price Perception Gap

Premium pricing without clear, communicated value justification creates friction at the point of purchase — especially among price-sensitive Gen Z consumers in growth markets.

Solution
✨

Value-Based Positioning

Instead of discounting, double down on storytelling: the craftsmanship behind each design, artist collaborations, and the quality that justifies the price. Introduce a tiered loyalty program for retention.

Problem
🎁

Weak Incentive Structure

Current customer incentives lack depth — no tiered loyalty program, no referral rewards, and limited UGC incentives to turn customers into brand advocates.

Solution
🏆

Layered Reward System

Build a 3-tier loyalty program with escalating benefits. Launch a referral program with discounts for both parties. Create a UGC rewards program where top community creators get featured and earn store credit.

Problem
📣

KOL-Heavy, Low Authenticity

Over-reliance on traditional KOL endorsements dilutes authenticity. Audiences, especially Gen Z, increasingly trust peer recommendations over polished influencer content.

Solution
💬

KOC + Story-Driven Content

Shift budget from macro-KOLs to Key Opinion Consumers (KOCs) — real users with genuine influence. Produce behind-the-scenes storytelling content: the ideation process, the artist's sketchbook, the factory floor. Emotion over promotion.

Marketing Mix

5Ps Strategy Framework

Every element of the marketing mix refined to close the gaps identified in the audit.

P

Product

Expand beyond phone cases into a full accessories ecosystem. Sustainability-first materials. Limited artist collaborations for scarcity and buzz.

P

Price

Maintain premium positioning ($40–$80 USD). Justify through storytelling, not discounting. Tiered loyalty pricing rewards repeat buyers.

P

Place

DTC-first through Casetify.com. Expand into China via Tmall, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and WeChat Mini Programs. Pop-up stores for physical brand immersion.

P

Promotion

KOC over KOL. Story-driven video content showing the creative process. Artist co-marketing campaigns. UGC challenges to spark organic reach.

P

People

Localize the marketing team for key regions. Deep partnerships with artists and designers. Build a community of super-users who shape product direction.

Brand Narrative

Communication: Emotion Over Promotion

The report identifies a key strategic shift: move from transactional marketing to emotional brand storytelling.

Strategy Shift
📖

Behind-the-Design Stories

Create video content that takes customers behind the scenes — from the first pencil sketch to the final product. Show the artist's process, the material selection, the factory craftsmanship. These stories deepen emotional connection and justify premium pricing without saying a word about price.

Channel Mix
📱

KOCs > Macro-KOLs

Partner with 50–100 nano-influencers (1K–50K followers) who genuinely love the brand rather than 5 celebrity KOLs. Their recommendations carry trust. Combine with a UGC challenge campaign that turns every customer into a potential brand ambassador.

Action Plan

Three-Phase Implementation

A phased rollout ensures each fix is validated before scaling.

Phase 1 — Diagnose & Research

Conduct deep-dive market research to validate the root causes of the Product, Service, and Pricing issues. Map out specific remediation plans with timelines and owners.

Phase 2 — Trial & Implement

Roll out pilot programs for the new Incentives structure and Communication strategy. Test KOC partnerships, launch the loyalty program beta, produce the first batch of behind-the-scenes content.

Phase 3 — Review & Scale

At end of 2024, conduct a full earnings review. Measure CSAT recovery, revenue growth, and market position. Set new targets for 2025 based on validated learnings.
